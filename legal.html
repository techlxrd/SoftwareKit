Privacy Policy

SoftwareKit aims to keep data handling to the minimum needed for specific features.

1. Data collection. SoftwareKit does not collect, track, sell, or share personal data for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

2. Device Info feature. If you use Device Info, some device-related information, including your UDID and technical details, may be sent temporarily to a Cloudflare Workers endpoint. This information is needed for the feature to work.

3. Temporary processing. Data sent to the Cloudflare Worker is stored temporarily for up to 5 minutes so the app can retrieve it. After that, it is automatically deleted and is not retained, logged, or reused.

4. Encryption. Data sent between the app and the Cloudflare Worker is encrypted in transit.

5. Local storage. Once the app receives your device information, it is stored locally on your device. It is not sent elsewhere again unless you manually use the Device Info feature.

6. Third-party services. Cloudflare Workers are used as a temporary processing layer, not for tracking, analytics, or data mining.
